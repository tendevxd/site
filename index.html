About me

I study creative production at an art institute and college of fine arts in Duhok. My training is in theater.

What sets me apart is that I build my own tools. When a project needs a phone that triggers live visuals, a bot that speaks in a voice call, or a 3D engine to test an idea, I write it myself. That lets me move between story, image, sound, and code.

My taste runs to 1960s rock and psychedelic looks, Tame Impala and Radiohead, and the music of Brazilian sertanejo and corridos tumbados. I write in Iraqi Arabic and English.
